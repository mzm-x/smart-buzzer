## 1. WHAT SMART BUZZER SELLS

Smart Buzzer delivers Google reviews to local business listings. Reviews are written by our team
based on the customer's business and services, then posted by real human reviewers on aged
accounts with unique IPs and devices.

**One-sentence description:**
> "We deliver Google reviews to your business listing — written for your services, posted gradually
> by real people, at a delivery speed you choose."

**Track record:** 1,200+ completed campaigns, 6+ years operating.

### What we do
* Write review content tailored to the customer's business (customer approves before posting)
* Post gradually at a customer-selected delivery speed
* Replace reviews that drop inside their 7-day window
* Provide a detailed delivery report

### What we do NOT do
* Remove or delete existing negative reviews
* Let the customer choose reviewer names
* Guarantee long-term permanence of any review
* Guarantee a specific star rating outcome
* Issue cash refunds (store voucher only — Section 4)

## 2. THE CORE CONCEPT — SHOW-UP REVIEWS

**This is the single most important concept in the product. Get it wrong and every downstream
number is wrong.**

The customer pays for reviews that **show up** on the listing. The moment a review appears, it
counts as **1 delivered**. We submit far more than the customer ordered, because only a portion of
submissions ever appear.

```
   ORDERED              SUBMITTED                SHOWS UP
   96 reviews    →    ~200-300 submits     →    96 counted
                      (we absorb the gap)       (what you pay for)
```

* A review counts when it **shows up** — not when it "sticks" long-term.
* Whether a review stays long-term is decided by Google's algorithm. Nobody controls that.
* The customer pays for results, not attempts.

> ⚠️ Internal: the older "we only count reviews that stick / ~3 stick per day" framing is
> **obsolete and contractually wrong**. Never use it. See Section 12.

## 3. DELIVERY SPEED — TWO TIERS

The customer selects a delivery speed per business during onboarding (`/submit/`). This drives the
whole timeline.

| Tier | Submits/day | Show-ups/day | Character |
|------|-------------|--------------|-----------|
| Standard | 5 | **2-3** | Slower, widest safety margin |
| Fast | 10 | **3-5** | Roughly 1.6× faster completion |

Both tiers stay within a natural, safe posting pattern. Reviews are staggered throughout the day.

### Timeline reference

Formula: `days = ceil(quantity ÷ show-ups per day) + 3` (the 3 days cover content prep + customer
approval before the first review appears).

| Order size | Standard (2-3/day) | Fast (3-5/day) |
|------------|--------------------|----------------|
| 72 | ~4-6 weeks | ~3-4 weeks |
| 96 | ~5-7 weeks | ~3-5 weeks |
| 100 | ~5-7 weeks | ~3-5 weeks |
| 132 | ~7-10 weeks | ~4-7 weeks |
| 200 | ~2-3 months | ~1-2 months |

**First reviews typically appear within 24 hours of content approval.** Total completion depends on
package size AND the tier selected — never quote a blanket "1-2 months".

## 4. GUARANTEE, DROPS & REFUNDS

### 4a. Drops are normal
Reviews can drop. This happens with organic reviews too, because of Google's algorithm. It is not a
defect in the service.

### 4b. 7-day per-review replacement window
Each review carries **its own 7-day replacement window, starting the day it appears.**

* Drops **inside** 7 days → replaced free. **One replacement per review.**
* Drops **after** 7 days → **final, NOT replaced.**
* If a replacement itself later drops → not replaced again.

We replace. We do **not** guarantee long-term permanence.

### 4c. Cases that do not count as a valid show-up (auto-replaced until filled)
| Case | Handling |
|------|----------|
| **Error names** — review lands from an Asian-server account | Does not count. Replaced automatically. |
| **Mistaken reviews** — wrong language or wrong rating | Cannot be removed, does not count. Free replacement until resolved. |

### 4d. Google wave updates
Google rolls out algorithm updates ("waves") a few times a year, usually around mid-year. Timing is
unpredictable and we get no advance notice. When a wave hits:

* Reviews still inside their 7-day window → covered by the standard per-review replacement.
* Reviews past their 7-day window → final, not replaced.
* The account stays safe.

### 4e. Claim period
Once a review's 7-day window closes, that review is final and **no further claims are accepted** for
it. Claims must be raised inside the window.

### 4f. Refund policy
* **Advance payment is required** before the campaign starts.
* **All sales are final. There are no cash refunds.**
* If a refund is approved, it is issued as a **store voucher** usable on any future order.
* **Once the campaign has started, the order is final** and cannot be cancelled or disputed.

```
1. QUALIFY          Business name + location → verify the Google Business Profile exists
2. CONTENT PREF     Customer supplies review content, or we write it
3. PROOF            Send 3 fresh maps.app.goo.gl links from recent delivered campaigns
                    ⚠️ Rotate these. Never reuse the same 3 links.
4. QUOTE            Package + price from the correct LP price list (Section 5)
5. PAYMENT          Customer picks method → send matching link or Zelle details
6. CONFIRM          Screenshot received → verify amount and handle → collect name + email
7. ONBOARDING       Send /submit/ form link
8. PREVIEW          Content preview sent → customer approves
9. DELIVERY         Posting begins; first reviews typically within 24h of approval
10. REPORT          Detailed delivery report provided
```

### Onboarding form (`/submit/`) collects
* Business name, business link, platform, country / state / industry
* Reviews allocated per business (multi-business orders supported)
* **Delivery speed** (the two tiers in Section 3)
* Rating option — 5-star only, or the 80/20 mix
* Review tone, review length, reviewer gender preference
* **15-20 detailed keywords minimum.** Example of the required detail level:
  *"Clean Place, Friendly & Satisfying Service, Easily Accessible Location, Customer Service is Very
  Helpful, Very Satisfying Products, Professional Staff, Great Value for Money"*
* TNC agreement (9 points, all must be acknowledged)

Post-order bonus shown on the success page: **free $20 voucher toward the customer's second purchase.**

8. **Guarantee & claim period.** Show-up plus 7-day per-review replacement — not permanence. After

9. **Refund policy.** Advance payment required. All sales final, no cash refunds. Approved refunds

## 11. FAQ

**Are these real?**
Real human reviewers on aged accounts with unique IPs and devices. Content is written around the
customer's services and approved by them before posting.

**Is it safe for my listing?**
Delivery is gradual and staggered, with a 4-star/5-star mix rather than a suspicious all-5-star
pattern. 1,200+ campaigns completed. Framing must stay "designed to stay compliant" — never a
zero-ban or 100%-safe claim.

**How long does it take?**
Depends on package size and the delivery speed selected. See the timeline table in Section 3. First
reviews typically appear within 24 hours of content approval.

**What if reviews drop?**
Each review has its own 7-day replacement window from the day it appears. Drops inside that window
are replaced free, one replacement per review. After 7 days a review is final.

**Can I get a refund?**
Once the campaign starts the order is final. There are no cash refunds. An approved refund is issued
as a store voucher usable on a future order.

**Can I choose the reviewer names?**
No. Content input yes, names no.

**Can I order for multiple businesses?**
Yes. Each business is quoted and configured separately, with its own review allocation and delivery
speed. Multi-business orders are delivered in parallel — the order completes when the slowest
business completes.

**Can you remove my negative reviews?**
No. We do not remove or delete reviews.

**Where is the team based?**
Remote team operating from Bali, Indonesia. Disclose honestly when asked.

| Banned | Use instead |

| "Zero drops" / "no drops" | "7-day per-review replacement" |

## 14. UNRESOLVED CONFLICTS — DO NOT GUESS

Each item below has two internal sources that disagree. Surface the conflict; do not pick a side.

### 14a. Reviewer name mix
* **LP pricing cards, UVP tables, and the `namesModal` on every landing page:** "(70%) Local +
  (30%) Global Names", with a modal explaining the split.
* **TNC point 7 (the signed contract):** names follow "a natural, worldwide spread (Global Names)".

These cannot both be true. Marketing advertises a controllable local-heavy split; the contract says
worldwide spread and that names cannot be selected. **A decision is needed before either side is
edited.**

### 14b. Minimum order amount
* **Sales playbook:** $300 USD hard minimum, no exceptions.
* **Product description / SKU copy:** $350 minimum purchase, and separately "a minimum of 55 reviews
  is required if buying Google Reviews only".

### 14c. Posting hours
* One source states **8am-8pm** US time per business location.
* Another states **9 AM - 9 PM** local business time, with minimum 2-4 hour intervals between posts.

Note also that the fast tier (up to 5 show-ups/day) inside a 12-hour window implies intervals
tighter than the stated 2-4 hour minimum. The interval figure may need loosening.

### 14d. Seasonal / Valentine pricing
Legacy pricing of 50 / 100 / 130 reviews at $300 / $550 / $650 (slugs `ojXkz`, `wK7vJ`, `BP9jx`)
circulated for direct chat sales. `/seasonal/` is a **rotating campaign slot** — the live campaign
changes and old slugs may be dead. Verify before quoting.

### 14e. Gateway coverage
