## PHASE 2 — DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT (Phone, ~10–15 min)

> Goal: Qualitative deep-dive, sales qualification, understand WHY they use each channel
> Audience: Only those who opted in for call (or warm leads you want to pursue)
> Format: Conversational — not a rigid form. These are your talking points.

---

### A — Client Profile (Warm Up, 2 min)

1. Can you tell me a bit about your business? What industry, how many locations?
2. How long have you been running paid marketing (ads, review services, etc.)?
3. Is marketing managed in-house, by an agency, or a mix of both?

---

### B — Marketing Spend Deep Dive (3 min)

4. What's your total monthly marketing budget across all channels combined?
5. For each channel — is the spend all-inclusive (service fee + ad spend together), or are they billed separately?
6. If separate: roughly what's the split between service fee vs. actual ad spend? (e.g., "I pay $500 agency fee + $1,500 ad budget")
7. Is your budget fixed monthly, or does it change based on the results your vendor delivers? (e.g., performance-based pricing)

---

### C — What Did You Buy & Why (2 min)

8. When you hire a marketing vendor, do you typically buy a package deal or pick services à la carte?
9. What specific services/deliverables were included in your last purchase? What did you think you were buying?
10. What made you choose that particular vendor? (e.g., they had a good portfolio, offered a free audit, cheaper, more credible, referred by someone)
11. Did you compare multiple vendors, or go with the first one that made sense?

---

### D — Channel Deep Dive (3 min)

12. For each channel you're paying for — who actually runs it day to day? Full vendor, internal team, or mixed?
13. What's the primary objective for each channel? (getting leads, building awareness, driving direct sales)
14. How many leads, calls, or sales per week/month do you need to hit your targets?
15. How do you currently measure success for each channel? Do you track cost per lead, ROAS, call volume?

---

### E — Pain Points & Current Reality (2 min)

16. What's the #1 thing preventing you from hitting your lead/sales goals right now?
17. Are you actively testing new vendors or new channels, or are you sticking with what you have?
18. If you are looking for something new — is it because your current setup isn't working, or you just want to scale on top of what's already working?
19. Have you ever had a bad experience with a marketing vendor? What happened?

---

### F — Google Reviews & Soft Sell (2 min)

20. How many Google reviews do you currently have, and what's your star rating?
21. If you could reliably add 5–10 new authentic-looking reviews per month — how do you think that would impact your leads or Maps ranking?
22. We currently help 1,200+ businesses do exactly that — gradual, natural review delivery. Would it make sense to walk you through how it works?
23. *(If interested)* What would you need to see to feel confident moving forward?

---

### Notes for Caller
- Questions 1–3: Listen more than talk. Build rapport.
- Questions 4–7: Key budget intel. Don't push — let them share.
- Questions 8–11: Understand buying behavior — this tells you how to pitch.
- Questions 16–19: Pain = opportunity. Listen for unresolved frustration.
- Questions 20–23: Natural segue into Smart Buzzer pitch. Only go here if trust is built.
- Keep it under 15 min. If they're engaged, book a follow-up for a full demo.
